name: weekly-review
description: Use every week (Sunday is the default), or when the founder says "weekly review", "review my week", "how did I do", or "scorecard". Scores the week against the north star, audits commitments kept versus missed, reviews due decisions, drills repeated English mistakes, and sets next week's one big thing.
argument-hint: "[week ending date]"
---

## Steps

1. Read the last seven `journal/` files, `me/commitments.md`, `me/north-star.md`, `me/scorecard.md`, and `decisions/log.md`.
2. **Numbers first.** Ask for this week's key numbers per business in one AskUserQuestion batch or a free-form reply: revenue booked, cash collected, receivables over 90 days, pipeline added, people hired or placed. Never guess them.
3. **Commitments audit.** Kept / missed / dropped, as a count and a percentage. For each miss, the real reason in one line. If the same kind of miss appears two weeks in a row, name the pattern.
4. **Scorecard.** Score the five pillars (Enterprise, Impact, Capital, Reputation, Self) from 1 to 10 with one line of evidence each. Append a row to `me/scorecard.md`.
5. **Policy and ecosystem pulse.** One line each: did anything change this week in MBG, procurement, or BUMN that affects the businesses? Did the businesses strengthen or squeeze the ecosystem (supplier payment days, safety incidents, people trained)? If the founder does not know, suggest `/policy-watch`.
6. **Decision review.** For each row in `decisions/log.md` whose review date has passed: ask what happened, fill Outcome and Lesson, and note whether the founder's confidence was well calibrated.
7. **English drill.** From the week's `English` lines, list the three most repeated mistakes, each with the correct form and two practice sentences in a business context. Suggest one IELTS task for the week (for example a Writing Task 2 essay on an MBG or entrepreneurship topic, or a Speaking Part 2 cue card).
8. **The mentor's verdict.** Under 150 words: what went well, what you are avoiding, and the **one big thing for next week**. Add it and up to two other commitments to `me/commitments.md`.
9. Commit and push.
