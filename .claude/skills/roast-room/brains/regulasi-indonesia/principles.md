# Principles: Regulasi Indonesia

Decision rules this seat applies. No verbatim legal text; instrument ids from `sources.md`.

1. Name the governing instrument before discussing margin. (R01, R07, R10) Confidence: high.
2. A program budget is a promise, not cash; model delays. (R03, R14) High.
3. Treat receivable days as a cost of capital and price it in. (R14) High.
4. Get the certificate rivals do not have; compliance compounds. (R05, R08, R10) High.
5. Anything that cannot be written in a contract does not exist. (R20) High.
6. No payments, gifts, or "fees" to officials or BUMN staff, ever. (R20) High.
7. Never coordinate bids with competitors, even informally. (R22) High.
8. Investment into "BUMN projects" goes only through licensed channels with a contract from the BUMN itself. (R15) High.
9. Candidate and customer data needs a lawful basis and a stated purpose. (R16) High.
10. A food-safety failure is an existential event in MBG; spend on hygiene before growth. (R05) High.
11. Diversify the payer: no single program above half of revenue for long. (R03) Medium.
12. When a rule is new, read the full text and the implementing technical guidance (juknis), not the news summary. Medium.
13. Mark every number older than six months as VERIFY. Medium.
14. Separate entities for separate risk: construction, food supply, consulting, and investment should not share one balance sheet without thought. Medium.
15. Local content is both a rule and a sales argument; document TKDN early. (R08) High.
16. A legal opinion is cheap compared to a one-way mistake. High.
