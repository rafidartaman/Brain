# Positions: Regulasi Indonesia

This seat's stance on common founder questions, in the Indonesian government-facing context.

| Topic | Position | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| Selling to MBG kitchens (SPPG) | Good market, but buyer quality varies. Sell to mitra with SLHS and a BGN agreement; get paid on delivery or with a deposit. | R02, R05 |
| Becoming an SPPG mitra yourself | High capex, one buyer (BGN), heavy food-safety liability. Only with strong operations and a hygiene system first. | R02, R04, R05 |
| E-katalog listing | Worth it for equipment. Needs TKDN and product data in order. Opens direct purchasing. | R07, R08 |
| Subcontracting to BUMN Karya | Revenue is real, cash is slow. Demand down payment and escrow where possible; check the BUMN's latest financials. | R14 |
| Loans to BUMN construction programs | Only through licensed channels, with the BUMN as the contractual counterparty. Otherwise treat as private credit risk. | R12, R14, R15 |
| MBG consulting | Legitimate if it sells knowledge and execution. Never sell "access" or "slots". Disclose conflicts when you also supply. | R20 |
| Head hunting for SPPG staff | Fine; protect candidate data, write clear contracts, check if placement licensing applies to your model. | R16, R17, R18 |
| Food supply chain to MBG | Strong thesis, needs cold chain and food safety; partner with cooperatives where possible. | R05, R21 |
| Debt | Acceptable for working capital against a signed government contract; dangerous against a promise. | R07, R14 |
| Partners | Put roles, money, and exits in a notarised agreement. Avoid partners whose value is "connections". | R20 |
| Political exposure | Programs tied to one administration need a plan for the next one. | R03, R06 |
| Taxes | Use the right regime per entity; government buyers withhold tax, so plan cash accordingly. | R19 |
| AI and data | Fine to use; candidate and student data falls under PDP. | R16 |
| Raising money | Investors will diligence licenses and concentration risk; fix both before pitching. | R10, R11 |
| Competition | Win on compliance and delivery record, never on price-fixing or "arrangements". | R22 |
