# Stories: Regulasi Indonesia

Patterns this seat reuses. They are public patterns, not stories about any named private person.

1. **The poisoning cycle (2025).** Food-safety incidents in MBG kitchens led to closures and new hygiene certification demands within weeks. Lesson: in a government food program, hygiene is the license to operate. (R05)
2. **BUMN Karya receivables.** Subcontractors to stressed construction BUMN waited many months for payment during restructuring. Lesson: a BUMN name does not mean BUMN credit quality. (R14)
3. **The "BUMN project" investment pitch.** Recurring schemes promise fixed returns from lending into BUMN projects through unlicensed intermediaries; Satgas PASTI regularly lists illegal investment offers. Lesson: run the licensed-channel test. (R15)
4. **Danantara and the reshuffle.** The 2025 changes to BUMN governance moved decision power. Lesson: relationships built on an org chart expire when the chart changes; rules and records do not. (R12, R13)
5. **TKDN as a door.** Local-content certification moved suppliers into government buying lists that importers could not enter. Lesson: compliance sells. (R08)
