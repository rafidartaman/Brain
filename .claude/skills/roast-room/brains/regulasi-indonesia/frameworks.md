# Frameworks: Regulasi Indonesia

Built from the instruments in `sources.md`. Scoring guidance is this seat's own, labeled as guidance.

## 1. Rule map
- **What:** a table for each option: instrument, agency, required license or certificate, status (have / missing / unknown), lead time.
- **Use when:** any deal with government, BUMN, or a regulated activity (food, construction, finance, labour).
- **Scoring guidance:** every "unknown" lowers the pillar score by 1; any missing license with lead time longer than the deadline caps the option at 4/10.
- **Sources:** R01, R07, R10, R11, R16.

## 2. Policy shock test
- **What:** three shocks per option. (a) Program budget cut 30% or payments delayed two quarters. (b) Rule tightened (new certificate, new standard). (c) Leadership change at the agency or BUMN.
- **Scoring guidance:** survives all three: 8 to 10. Survives two: 5 to 7. Survives one or none: 4 or lower, and flag RUIN RISK possible if the founder has debt or guarantees attached.
- **Sources:** R03, R05, R06, R13.

## 3. Government cash cycle
- **What:** receivable days (assume 90 to 180 for BUMN Karya subcontracts, 30 to 90 for direct government after acceptance), performance bond (jaminan pelaksanaan), retention, down payment (uang muka) rules.
- **Use when:** construction, equipment supply, any termin-based contract.
- **Scoring guidance:** if working capital needed exceeds 6 months of the founder's free cash, score 3 or lower unless financing is committed.
- **Sources:** R07, R14.

## 4. Integrity line
- **What:** for each option, list every payment, gift, fee, or "success fee" that touches an official or a BUMN employee, and every arrangement with competitors in a tender.
- **Scoring guidance:** any item that fails is a hard no (ruin risk likely). Reputation and criminal exposure are irreversible.
- **Sources:** R20, R22.

## 5. Compliance moat ladder
- **What:** rank certificates and registrations by (time to obtain) x (how many rivals lack it). Get the top one first.
- **Examples:** SLHS and HACCP for kitchens, TKDN certificate and e-katalog listing for equipment, SBU at the right grade (kualifikasi) for construction, OJK-licensed channel for lending.
- **Sources:** R05, R08, R10, R15.

## 6. Licensed-channel test (investments)
- **What:** for any loan or investment "into a BUMN project": who is the legal borrower, is the channel OJK-licensed, is there a signed contract from the BUMN itself, where does the money sit, what is the collateral.
- **Scoring guidance:** if the borrower is a private intermediary with only a BUMN letter, treat it as unsecured private credit, not a BUMN exposure.
- **Sources:** R12, R14, R15.

## 7. Data and people compliance (head hunting)
- **What:** consent and purpose for candidate data (PDP), contract type (PKWT, outsourcing), whether placement needs an LPTKS license.
- **Sources:** R16, R17, R18.

## 8. Tax structure check
- **What:** which entity bills, which tax regime applies (final MSME rate versus normal), VAT on government sales, withholding by government treasurers.
- **Sources:** R19. Always VERIFY with a tax consultant.
