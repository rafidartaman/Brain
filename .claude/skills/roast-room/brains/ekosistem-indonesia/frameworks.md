# Frameworks: Ekosistem Indonesia

## 1. Chain map (hulu to hilir)
- Draw each actor, what flows (goods, money, data, trust), payment days, and where value is lost.
- MBG example: petani/peternak/nelayan, koperasi or pengepul, distributor, SPPG mitra, sekolah, siswa and orang tua, BGN. (E02, E03)
- Scoring guidance: an option that fixes a broken link (late payment, spoilage, quality) scores high; one that adds a middle layer without value scores low.

## 2. Stakeholder grid
- Power versus interest. High power, high interest (BGN, pemda, BUMN owner): manage closely. Low power, high interest (farmers, workers, parents): protect, because their anger becomes headlines. (E07)

## 3. Shared value test
- Does business profit rise when the social outcome improves (healthier kids, better-paid farmers, safer sites)? If yes, the model is aligned. If profit rises when outcomes fall, the model is fragile. (E06)

## 4. Fragility scan
- Count concentration: share of revenue from the largest buyer, share of supply from the largest supplier, number of key relationships that are one person. Above 50% on any is a red flag. (E08)

## 5. Impact scorecard
- Three indicators, tracked monthly, reported honestly. Examples: portions delivered with zero safety incidents, local suppliers paid within 14 days, people hired or placed, workers certified (SKK, food handler), rupiah spent locally.

## 6. Upstream opportunity scan
- Where is the ecosystem weakest: aggregation, cold chain, quality grading, working capital for small suppliers, training? The weakest link is often the best business. (E03, E09)

## 7. Exit-and-dependency check
- If you leave, who is stranded? Build options that leave capacity behind (trained people, organised cooperatives), so leaving is not destruction.
