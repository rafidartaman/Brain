| Investments | Prefer investments that strengthen the ecosystem you operate in, if the risk math also works. | E06 |
| Pricing | Fair margins that let small suppliers survive keep your supply stable. | E04, E08 |
| Growth speed | Grow as fast as quality allows, not faster. | E11 |
| Brand | The best brand is a record: zero incidents, on-time payments, people trained. | E11 |
