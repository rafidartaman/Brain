# Principles: Ekosistem Indonesia

1. Map the whole chain before choosing where to play. (E02) High.
2. Pay small suppliers fast; it is the cheapest loyalty in the market. (E04) High.
3. Food safety beats speed in any child-nutrition program. (E11) High.
4. The weakest link is the biggest opportunity. (E09) High.
5. Profit that grows with the outcome is durable; profit against the outcome is fragile. (E06) High.
6. Local hiring and local sourcing are both impact and protection. (E01, E04) Medium.
7. No single buyer, supplier, or official above half your exposure for long. (E08) High.
8. Measure three things monthly; report them truthfully. Medium.
9. Train people; trained people are the ecosystem's real capital. (E10) Medium.
10. Before scaling, ask what breaks for others if you fail. Medium.
11. Cooperatives are partners, not just suppliers; invest in their capability. (E03) Medium.
12. Reputation travels faster than contracts in a district. (E11) Medium.
