# Stories: Ekosistem Indonesia

Public patterns, not stories about named private people.

1. **Spoiled produce.** Fresh produce is often lost between farm and market for lack of cold chain and grading. Lesson: upstream logistics is an open opportunity. (E09)
2. **Late payments and supplier exit.** When kitchens pay small suppliers late, suppliers quietly leave or cut quality. Lesson: payment speed is supply security. (E04, E08)
3. **Food safety headlines.** Incidents in 2025 hurt trust in the whole program, not just the kitchens involved. Lesson: the reputation pool is shared. (E11)
4. **Cooperatives as nodes.** The Merah Putih cooperative push created thousands of new entities that need buyers, training, and working capital. Lesson: be the partner that makes them work. (E03)
