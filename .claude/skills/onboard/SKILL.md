## What this does

Turns "become the most successful youth entrepreneur in Indonesia" into numbers, dates, and a profile the mentor and the council can rely on.

## Steps

1. Read `me/profile.md`, `me/north-star.md`, and `me/commitments.md`. List the TODOs.
2. Interview in batches with AskUserQuestion (at most four questions per batch, at most five batches). Order:
   - **Batch 1, the founder:** age, base city, hours per week, the target date for the north star.
   - **Batch 2, each business:** monthly revenue, margin, team size, top two customers or payers. One batch per two businesses if needed. Offer ranges as options (for example "under Rp100 juta / Rp100 to 500 juta / Rp500 juta to 2 miliar / above") so answering is fast; "Other" lets them type exact numbers.
   - **Batch 3, cash and risk:** cash on hand, monthly fixed costs, debt and guarantees, receivables over 90 days, details of each BUMN loan investment.
   - **Batch 4, the north star:** confirm or rewrite the draft definition; pick 12-month targets per pillar; the quarter's one big thing.
   - **Batch 5, self:** current IELTS estimate and test date, health habits.
   If the founder prefers to type freely, accept a free-form dump and extract the numbers yourself.
3. Write the answers into `me/profile.md` and `me/north-star.md`. Keep the file structure. Mark estimates as `(est.)`. Leave a TODO only where the founder chose not to answer.
4. Mentor read-back (under 200 words): the three biggest risks you see in the profile (look especially at policy concentration, BUMN counterparty exposure shared by the construction business and loan investments, receivables, and any conflict between consulting and supply), and the one target that looks too low or too high, with a reason.
5. Add up to three commitments to `me/commitments.md` that start the first week.
6. Commit and push (cloud containers are deleted after a session).

## Rules

- Never invent a number. An unknown stays TODO.
- English check applies (see `CLAUDE.md`) to the founder's typed answers.
