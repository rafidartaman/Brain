# Decision log

Every significant decision, with your prediction, so you learn whether your judgement is getting better. The mentor reviews each line on its review date and asks: what happened, and what did I miss?

Format: one row per decision. `Confidence` is how sure you were that the call would work (0 to 100%).

| Date | Decision | The call | Source (council path or "mentor") | My prediction | Confidence | Review date | Outcome | Lesson |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
